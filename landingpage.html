Four Siblings, One House

Where It Started

Maasha Amor began at a kitchen table, not a boardroom. Four siblings shared one belief: clothing should hold something of you, not just cover you.

What began as gifts for family became a house that treats every order the same way it treated the first one — like it matters to someone.

"Amor" means love. We put it in the name so we'd never forget why we started.

— The Founders
